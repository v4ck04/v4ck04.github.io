Small business · invoicing

Izdavanje računa

Invoices written out by hand and VAT worked out on a calculator — with no quick way to answer how much did we turn over last month. This does the arithmetic, prints the invoice, and keeps the totals.

Item catalogue — add, edit and remove what you sell, each with its own VAT rate
Invoice built from items and quantities, with net, VAT and total recalculated on every change
Print preview of the finished invoice before it goes to paper
Reports over a chosen date range: invoice count, net, VAT and total turnover
Each invoice written inside a transaction, so a half-saved invoice can't exist
Line items keep their own name, price and VAT rate, so an old invoice stays accurate after a price change
Every query parameterised, closing off SQL injection
Three-layer structure — models, data access, forms — kept separate

Built with C# · .NET Framework 4.8 · WinForms · SQL Server · ADO.NET
